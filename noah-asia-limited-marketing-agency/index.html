EMF Workflow Pain Points

Potential gaps based on Noah's event, exhibition, roadshow, and brand activation work

Pre-event

  • Client research may be scattered across websites, LinkedIn, past project notes, and email threads.
  • Proposal, quotation, and creative copy preparation can become time-consuming when every brand activation needs a different angle.
  • Promoter, supplier, and venue coordination may create repeated status-checking work before the event.
  • New brand outreach may be less effective if emails are too generic for different sectors such as retail, electronics, malls, and corporate clients.

During-event

  • On-site client requests and urgent updates can be hard to summarize quickly for the sales, project, and execution teams.
  • Guest reception, media arrangements, and repeated enquiries may consume experienced staff time.
  • Live event content may not be converted into usable social posts, recap copy, or follow-up material quickly enough.
  • Internal handover may be incomplete if key client comments stay in chat messages or personal notes.

Post-event

  • Follow-up emails may be delayed because teams need to first organize photos, client feedback, and event results.
  • Feedback and reporting can require manual collection from multiple sources.
  • Photos, videos, and event notes may not be fully repurposed into case studies or business development content.
  • Future sales conversations may lack a structured summary of client interests, objections, and next opportunities.
